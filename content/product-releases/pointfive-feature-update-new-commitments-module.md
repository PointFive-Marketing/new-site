Our new commitments module is designed to provide unparalleled visibility into existing AWS commitments across all services, accounts, and regions from a single pane of glass. Comprehensive visibility, coupled with features like effective savings rate (ESR), timely notifications, and utilization insights, enables customers to more effectively manage their AWS commitments and recognize greater cost savings.
