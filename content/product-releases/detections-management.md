Fine-tune how PointFive detects cloud waste with granular control over detection rules, thresholds, and exclusions.

## Customize Detection Rules

Adjust detection sensitivity and thresholds to match your organization's cloud usage patterns. Set custom parameters for different services, accounts, or resource types to reduce false positives.

## Manage Exclusions

Easily exclude specific resources or accounts from detection when you know they require special handling. Keep your optimization pipeline focused on actionable opportunities.

## Full Visibility

Track which detections are active, paused, or customized across your environment. Maintain a clear audit trail of detection configuration changes.
