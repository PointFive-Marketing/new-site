The latest update to the tracking system allows you to treat opportunities as tickets which ensures transparent handling and monitoring of opportunities from detection to resolution. By treating opportunities as tickets, activity is tracked to keep stakeholders informed of progress and remaining tasks, enabling a more collaborative approach to the process and ensuring accountability across the team. Additionally, by leveraging logs from the cloud provider, users can identify who created or who is remotely connected to the resource.
