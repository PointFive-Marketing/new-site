PointFive's agentless Kubernetes observability provides full cost visibility across EKS clusters, enabling teams to optimize K8s spending without installing any agents.

## Completely Agentless

Unlike traditional Kubernetes cost tools, PointFive requires no agents, sidecars, or DaemonSets. Leverage native cloud provider capabilities to access comprehensive performance metrics without additional software.

## Pre-built and Custom Dashboards

Access all managed K8s clusters across cloud environments with customizable views. Visualize costs at the cluster, namespace, and workload level.

## Enhanced Cloud Oversight for Cloud Ops Engineers

Drill down to the Kubernetes level for understanding resource costs and identifying inefficiencies. See how K8s workloads map to underlying cloud infrastructure costs.

## Streamline Cost Management

Unified observation of cloud and Kubernetes costs together provides a complete picture of infrastructure spending, eliminating blind spots that lead to waste.
