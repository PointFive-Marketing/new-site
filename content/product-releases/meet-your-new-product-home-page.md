We're excited to introduce a brand-new experience just for you — the Home Page for Team Members.

Here's what you'll find when you log in:

- A clear view of your assigned opportunities
- Your team's progress and potential savings
- How fast you're resolving issues with time to resolution insights
- A list of new opportunities since your last login — so you always know where to start

## Why It Matters

You can now track your impact, celebrate progress, and take action right from the moment you log in. It's built for your daily flow — simple, focused, and powerful.
