The enhanced Group By feature adds multi-level grouping, tag-based grouping, and cross-account analysis capabilities for deeper cost and usage investigation.

## What's New

- **Multi-Level Grouping** — Stack multiple dimensions to drill into cost drivers at granular levels
- **Tag-Based Grouping** — Group by custom tags to align cost views with your organizational structure
- **Cross-Account Analysis** — Compare spending patterns across accounts and identify outliers

## Why It Matters

Understanding where cloud costs originate requires flexible analysis tools. The enhanced Group By feature helps teams slice data from multiple angles simultaneously, accelerating root cause investigation and cost attribution.
