PointFive's enhanced remediation capabilities now include guided workflows, automated execution options, and progress tracking for faster cloud waste resolution.

## Guided Workflows

Step-by-step remediation guides help engineers take the right action for each optimization opportunity. Clear instructions reduce the risk of misconfiguration and speed up resolution.

## Automated Execution

For supported resource types, execute remediation actions directly from the PointFive platform. One-click fixes eliminate the need to switch between tools.

## Progress Tracking

Monitor remediation progress across your organization. Track which opportunities have been addressed, which are in progress, and where savings have been realized.
