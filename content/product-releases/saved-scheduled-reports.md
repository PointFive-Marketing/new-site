We've just made reporting a whole lot easier — no more reapplying filters every time or exporting manually. Your reports now work for you.

## Key Features

- **Save reports with filters** — Keep your favorite views handy
- **Schedule delivery** — Daily, weekly, or monthly via Email or Slack
- **Download as PDF** — Each scheduled report includes a downloadable PDF version and a link to the dashboard
- **Edit reports** — Rename, re-schedule, or delete — you're in control
