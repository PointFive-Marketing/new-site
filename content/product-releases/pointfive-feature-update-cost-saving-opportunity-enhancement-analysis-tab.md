Our analytics capabilities go even deeper with the new analysis tab available on every cost savings opportunity page. The insights provided through the analysis tab offer deeper insights into specific resource usage metrics and detailed cost breakdowns. With this enhancement, users gain full context for the opportunity, and a clear understanding of why it was detected and the best way to realize its savings.
