Users can now "favorite" specific dashboards with the new Analytics - Favorites feature. By marking a dashboard as a favorite, it will automatically appear at the top of the user's dashboard list. This will help users quickly access the most relevant insights tailored to their needs without the need to hunt through a long list of dashboards.
