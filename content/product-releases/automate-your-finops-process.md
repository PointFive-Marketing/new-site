PointFive now offers powerful automation capabilities that transform cloud cost management from reactive to proactive. Teams can build custom, no-code workflows that trigger actions across their existing toolchain.

## Custom Workflows, No Code Required

Create automated workflows that connect PointFive insights to actions across Slack, Jira, email, and cloud provider APIs. Define triggers based on cost thresholds, anomaly detection, or new optimization opportunities.

## End-to-End Automation

From detection to remediation, automate the entire FinOps lifecycle. When a new savings opportunity is identified, automatically create a Jira ticket, notify the right team via Slack, and track progress through resolution.

## Built for Teams

Automation workflows are designed for collaboration. Assign ownership, set escalation paths, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks — all without manual intervention.
