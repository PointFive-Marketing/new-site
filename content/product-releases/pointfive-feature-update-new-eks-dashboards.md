Our EKS solution enables actionable cost-saving strategies in the Kubernetes (K8s) environment and underlying cloud infrastructure. As a result of our completely agentless approach, our solution offers a 360° view of the K8s clusters for deep cost analysis making all resources visible for comprehensive cost observability at both application and infrastructure levels. This removes the need for engineers to browse through various tools and conduct manual research to gather the data points they need to take action.
