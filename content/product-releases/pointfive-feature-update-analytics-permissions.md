Permissions, a new feature of Analytics enables administrators to control access to sensitive dashboard data within the PointFive platform. Administrators can define view permissions on a per-dashboard basis so that only those who need the information have access to it. Permissions can be configured at the individual user level and the team level, enabling admins to easily align access with organizational roles and responsibilities.
