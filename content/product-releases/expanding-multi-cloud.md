PointFive expands its multi-cloud support with deeper Azure and GCP coverage, delivering unified cloud cost optimization across all major providers.

## What's New

- **Deeper Azure Coverage** — Expanded detection and optimization for Azure compute, storage, and database services
- **GCP Enhancements** — New support for GCP-specific resource types and commitment models
- **Unified Dashboard** — View and compare optimization opportunities across AWS, Azure, and GCP from a single pane of glass

## Why It Matters

As organizations adopt multi-cloud strategies, maintaining visibility and control over spending across providers becomes increasingly complex. PointFive's expanded multi-cloud support ensures teams can optimize costs regardless of where workloads run.
