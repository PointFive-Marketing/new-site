Our updates to notification rules ensure that users can handle and respond to saving opportunities and ensure new opportunities are noticed on time. We provide a user-friendly list of rules, each tailored with specific triggers and actions, to guarantee that every new opportunity is swiftly attended to by the most suitable person. Additionally, with intelligent notification, we help you to prioritize savings opportunities by effort and risk.
