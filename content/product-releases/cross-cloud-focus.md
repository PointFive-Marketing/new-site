PointFive now supports FOCUS billing data across AWS, Azure, and GCP — delivering unified, normalized cost visibility across all major cloud providers.

## What's New

- **AWS, Azure & GCP** — FOCUS-based billing ingestion is now available for all three major cloud providers
- **Unified Cost Views** — Compare and analyze spending across providers using a single, consistent data model
- **Deeper Insights** — Leverage FOCUS-normalized data for more accurate cross-cloud cost attribution and optimization

## Why It Matters

Managing costs across multiple cloud providers has traditionally required reconciling different billing formats. With FOCUS support across all three clouds, teams can now view and analyze spending through a single lens.
