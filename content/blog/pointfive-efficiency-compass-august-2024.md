Welcome to the August 2024 edition of the PointFive Efficiency Compass — our monthly roundup of cloud optimization insights, product updates, and industry trends.

## What's New This Month

### Platform Updates

August saw significant enhancements to our detection engine, with expanded coverage for Azure and GCP services alongside our established AWS capabilities. Our analytics module received updates to support FOCUS-standard data exports.

### Industry Developments

The FinOps community continued to grow through the summer, with FinOps X 2024 driving new conversations around engineering-driven cost optimization. The release of FOCUS 1.0 set new standards for cross-cloud billing data normalization.

### Customer Success

We're proud to share continued momentum with enterprise customers achieving meaningful savings through PointFive's DeepWaste Detection Engine. More case studies and success stories coming soon.
