## From Foundation to Scale

PointFive was built to reveal concealed cost inefficiencies in cloud environments and bring engineering teams into the waste reduction process. While 2024 focused on establishing the foundational elements of the platform and go-to-market motion, the company positions 2025 as the year of scale.

Leadership acknowledges that market confusion about PointFive's differentiation has been a challenge -- but notes that clarity consistently emerges during product demonstrations. The 2025 strategy therefore prioritizes getting in front of more potential customers directly.

## Boots on the Ground

The company is committing to increased visibility across several channels:

- **Partner Collaboration:** Expanding relationships with selective partners who possess strong technical and FinOps capabilities across regions
- **Events Expansion:** Growing presence at FinOps and DevOps community events and conferences throughout the year
- **Direct Engagement:** Gathering practitioner feedback on needed improvements and building relationships with the people who live in cloud cost data every day

## Platform Advancements

**Multi-Cloud Maturity:** By the end of January 2025, Azure and GCP support will reach maturity, complementing existing deep AWS capabilities. This positions PointFive to serve complex environments where organizations run workloads across multiple providers.

**New and Upcoming Features:**

- Real-time cost anomaly detection that addresses the inherent lag issues in traditional alerting
- Built-in root-cause analysis functionality to accelerate investigation
- ML-driven DeepWaste Detection for surfacing increasingly sophisticated inefficiency patterns
- LLM and Co-pilot tools for accelerated infrastructure and cost research
- Advanced anomaly detection algorithms that reduce false positives

## Making FinOps Engaging

The company is also focusing on making cloud cost optimization less tedious and more approachable, planning creative campaigns to shift perception of FinOps work from a chore to a high-impact engineering discipline.

## Looking Ahead

PointFive plans to expand beyond pure cost data, integrating adjacent disciplines into a unified platform experience. The 2025 approach aims to bring engineers closer to FinOps than ever before, motivating teams to maintain infrastructure efficiency as a continuous practice rather than a periodic exercise.
