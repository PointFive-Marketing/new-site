## Reflecting on re:Invent 2025

PointFive participated at AWS re:Invent 2025, where many of the teams visiting the booth shared that they are prioritizing real-world AWS cost efficiency. The conversations revealed common themes across organizations of all sizes, with several recurring concerns emerging from the discussions.

## What Teams Are Focused On

Engineering and FinOps teams highlighted a number of persistent cost challenges they face on AWS, including:

- **EC2 sizing gaps** -- instances provisioned beyond actual workload requirements
- **EBS bloat** -- over-allocated or orphaned storage volumes driving up monthly bills
- **Data transfer blind spots** -- cross-region and cross-AZ traffic costs that are difficult to trace
- **Hidden service costs** -- lesser-known AWS services generating unexpected charges

These issues are common across organizations running production workloads at scale, and they underscore the need for deeper visibility and actionable guidance.

## Cloud Efficiency Posture Management

PointFive's approach to tackling these challenges is through **Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM)**, which provides continuous monitoring, trustworthy insights, and prioritized recommendations that teams can take directly to engineering.

The overarching goal discussed at the booth was building more efficiently in AWS -- making cloud spend work for the business rather than the other way around.

## Looking Ahead

re:Invent 2025 reinforced that the industry is moving beyond simple cost visibility toward proactive, engineering-driven cloud optimization. PointFive remains committed to helping teams close the gap between knowing about waste and actually eliminating it.
