## Fast Answers Without Friction

Cloud cost questions rarely have simple answers. Understanding why spending spiked last week, which team owns an underutilized resource, or what the safest way to rightsize a database instance might be -- these questions typically require navigating multiple tools, chasing stakeholders, and piecing together context from different sources.

Pointer, PointFive's AI assistant for cloud cost optimization, is built to eliminate that friction. Teams can ask questions in plain English and get validated savings insights with the full technical context required to understand impact and take action.

## Complete Answers, Not Partial Data

What sets Pointer apart from generic chatbot interfaces is the depth and completeness of its responses. Each answer includes:

- **Clear Explanations** -- Not just numbers, but the reasoning behind recommendations, making it easy to understand why a particular optimization matters
- **Ownership Mapping** -- Identification of which team, account, or service owns the affected resources, removing the guesswork about who needs to act
- **Actionable Guidance** -- Specific next steps for implementation, including technical details about what to change and how

This approach eliminates the follow-up queries that typically plague cost investigations. Instead of getting a partial answer and needing to ask five more questions, Pointer delivers comprehensive responses upfront.

## From Questions to Decisions

Pointer removes the need to search documentation or chase the right stakeholders. Teams can move from cloud cost questions to confident, savings-driven decisions as part of their continuous cloud efficiency practice. Whether the question comes from a FinOps practitioner reviewing monthly spend or an engineer investigating a specific resource, Pointer provides the same level of validated, contextual insight.
