## Introducing the Cloud Efficiency Hub

The Cloud Efficiency Hub launched as a collaborative initiative focused on compiling shared cloud inefficiencies into a comprehensive public knowledge base. The Hub brings together over 70 contributors to catalog more than 200 cloud inefficiencies, establishing itself as a critical resource for engineering and FinOps teams within the expanding Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM) category.

## Why the Hub Matters

Cloud infrastructure growth has increased complexity, frequently producing hidden inefficiencies that create unnecessary costs and operational performance issues. Traditional approaches to identifying waste often depend on individual team knowledge, leading to inconsistent coverage and missed opportunities.

The Hub addresses this challenge through collaborative methods, drawing on practitioners from major organizations to build a shared catalog of cloud waste patterns, their root causes, and recommended remediation approaches.

## Contributor Perspectives

**Matt Walls, FinOps Engineer at NBCUniversal (Founding Contributor):**
The Hub centralizes knowledge around cloud inefficiencies in a way that has not been done before. It provides an opportunity to learn from global experts and share experiences optimizing cloud environments.

**Christine Oji, Cloud FinOps Engineer (Founding Contributor):**
By documenting common waste patterns and their drivers, the Hub reinforces the value of collective knowledge in advancing cloud efficiency practices.

## Governance and Structure

The project operates through a transparent governance structure including:

- **Contributors** who submit and review inefficiency documentation
- **Maintainers** who ensure content quality and consistency
- **Governing Board** that provides strategic direction and community alignment

This structure ensures content accuracy while keeping the project community-driven and accessible.

## About the Cloud Efficiency Hub

The Cloud Efficiency Hub is an open-source community initiative focused on identifying and eliminating cloud inefficiencies. Built by cloud practitioners for practitioners, it offers actionable insights and optimization best practices.
