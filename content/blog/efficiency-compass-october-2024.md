Welcome to the October 2024 edition of the PointFive Efficiency Compass — our monthly roundup of cloud optimization insights, product updates, and industry trends.

## What's New This Month

### Platform Updates

October brought key improvements to PointFive's platform, including enhanced multi-cloud analytics and new automation capabilities for common remediation workflows. Teams can now track optimization progress with improved team-level attribution and reporting.

### Industry Trends

With AWS re:Invent approaching, the cloud industry is gearing up for a wave of new service announcements and pricing updates. FinOps practitioners are preparing to evaluate new commitment models and optimization opportunities that these announcements will bring.

### Series A Milestone

We're excited to share that PointFive recently secured $20M in Series A funding led by Salesforce Ventures, bringing our total capital to $36M. This investment accelerates our multi-cloud capabilities and deepens our DeepWaste Detection Engine.
