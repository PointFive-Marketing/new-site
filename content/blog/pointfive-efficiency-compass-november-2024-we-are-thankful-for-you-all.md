Welcome to the November 2024 edition of the PointFive Efficiency Compass! As we approach Thanksgiving, we want to express our gratitude to the incredible community of FinOps practitioners, cloud engineers, and customers who make our work meaningful.

## What We're Thankful For

### Our Growing Community

2024 has been a transformative year for PointFive and the broader cloud efficiency community. We're thankful for every team that has trusted us to help them find and fix cloud waste — and for the FinOps practitioners who continue to raise the bar for what cloud efficiency looks like.

### AWS re:Invent 2024

With re:Invent just around the corner, we're excited to connect with the cloud community in Las Vegas. Look for the PointFive team at the event — we'll be sharing insights on DeepWaste Detection and our vision for Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM).

### Looking Ahead

As we close out 2024, we're energized by the momentum in our product, our team, and the industry. Stay tuned for exciting announcements in the new year.
