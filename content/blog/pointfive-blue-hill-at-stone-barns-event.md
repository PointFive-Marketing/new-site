## An Evening at Blue Hill at Stone Barns

PointFive hosted an evening gathering at the renowned Blue Hill at Stone Barns in Westchester, New York. The event brought together FinOps practitioners and cloud professionals for an evening of exceptional food and engaging conversation.

## Highlights

Attendees shared professional insights and best practices throughout the evening. Key discussion topics included:

- Cloud cost optimization strategies and emerging approaches
- Building stronger connections across the FinOps community
- Envisioning future developments in the FinOps field

The event fostered new professional connections and reinforced the collaborative spirit that drives the FinOps community forward.

## Looking Ahead

Thank you to everyone who joined for the evening. PointFive looks forward to welcoming attendees at future events.
