## PointFive Efficiency Compass -- December 2024

As 2024 draws to a close, PointFive reflects on a year of rapid growth and significant accomplishments. The Efficiency Compass newsletter captures the momentum the company has built since emerging from stealth.

## A Year of Firsts

2024 was a defining year for PointFive. The company launched publicly in June with $16M in seed funding led by Index Ventures, followed by a $20M Series A round led by Salesforce Ventures in November -- bringing total funding to $36M within 18 months of inception.

The pace of execution extended across every dimension of the business: expanding the customer base to include enterprise brands, deepening AWS service coverage, and beginning the multi-cloud journey with GCP and Azure support development.

## Platform Growth and Impact

Throughout the year, PointFive's DeepWaste Detection Engine continued to surface hidden cloud inefficiencies for customers, consistently identifying savings representing 15-30% of total cloud spend. The platform expanded its coverage across key AWS services and began laying the foundation for multi-cloud analytics powered by the FOCUS specification.

The 5x Research Team played a central role in this progress, continuously mapping new inefficiency patterns and translating their findings into automated detections available to every customer.

## Looking Ahead

With a strong foundation established in 2024, the team enters 2025 focused on scaling -- expanding multi-cloud capabilities to full maturity, growing the partner ecosystem, and introducing AI-driven features to make cloud cost optimization faster and more accessible. The year ahead represents an opportunity to build on the momentum and deliver even greater impact for customers navigating increasingly complex cloud environments.
