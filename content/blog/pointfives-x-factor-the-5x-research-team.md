## What Is the 5x Research Team?

PointFive created the 5x Research Team to address a critical gap: organizations need dedicated cloud inefficiency hunters who go beyond surface-level analysis. The team is a specialized group of FinOps and cloud researchers who dive into complex billing data, usage patterns, and infrastructure configurations to uncover savings opportunities that automated tools alone would miss.

The team operates around three primary guidelines: **accuracy, actionability, and impact**. These principles ensure that the overwhelming volume of potential insights from cloud environments gets curated into findings that customers can act on immediately and that drive meaningful cost reductions.

Their research is molded directly into the PointFive platform, so every customer benefits from the team's discoveries with minimal effort.

## Why an Internal Research Team?

An internal research team at a cloud cost optimization company may seem unconventional, but it reflects PointFive's cybersecurity heritage. CEO Alon Arvatz and the leadership team come from a security background where staying ahead of evolving threats is the core discipline.

In the context of cloud cost optimization, the 5x Team treats inefficiencies the same way a security team treats vulnerabilities. Instead of security hygiene, they focus on cost hygiene -- applying the same depth, speed, accuracy, and actionability that works in security. This approach helps customers avoid alert fatigue and false-positive floods while systematically reducing the surface area of cost waste.

## What the Team Produces

The 5x Team's work takes several forms:

- **Saving Opportunities** surfaced through the DeepWaste Detection Engine
- **Analytics Insights** delivered via the Cloud Intelligence interface
- **Ad-hoc Projects** based on specific customer requests and environments
- **Professional Services Engagements** for deeper, customized analysis

In one notable engagement, the team worked with a customer spending hundreds of millions annually on cloud infrastructure. They detected novel inefficiency patterns that had gone undetected for years, ultimately leading to millions of dollars in savings.

## How the Team Scales

What makes the 5x Team a true differentiator is the scalability model. Each finding the team produces for one customer is immediately replicated and extended to the entire customer base through PointFive's architecture. Think of it as a virtual FinOps team that every customer gets access to.

This is part of how the team got its name -- "5x" implies both a multiplier on the value customers receive from the platform and the network effect of the team's findings. A detection created for one environment is automatically applied across all customer environments, creating compounding value.

## The Case for Specialist Research

Rather than investing significant resources to build an in-house team of cloud cost researchers, organizations can leverage the 5x Research Team's accumulated expertise. The team works continuously to surface new saving opportunities through the PointFive platform, ensuring that customers benefit from the latest research and detection capabilities as they become available.

The combination of human expertise and automated detection creates a feedback loop: researchers identify new patterns, those patterns get encoded into the platform, and the platform's broad coverage across customer environments surfaces additional edge cases for the researchers to investigate.
