## Day 2 at FinOps X 2024

The second day of the FinOps X Conference 2024 continued building on the momentum established on Day 1, delivering detailed insights through keynotes and panels from industry leaders.

## Integrating Finance and Technology at American Express

Keena Blunschi (CFO for Enterprise Services) and Noreen Dever (platform economics lead) from American Express demonstrated how combining finance and technology strengthens cloud cost management. Key points from their session:

- FinOps has become a topic in senior executive meetings, reflecting leadership's focus on cloud adoption
- Real-time cost monitoring enabled the team to quickly identify and address a cost spike during a data migration
- Collaboration between finance and technology teams is essential as organizations progress through FinOps maturity phases

## Evolution of FinOps Tooling

Mike Fuller, CTO of the FinOps Foundation, spoke about the evolution of FinOps tooling and ecosystem integration. His central message was that while custom-built tools offer benefits for specific needs, leveraging the existing ecosystem of FinOps tools can streamline cost management implementation. Organizations benefit from integrating both native cloud tools and tailored solutions that align with their unique requirements.

## Cloud Provider FinOps Updates

### Google Cloud

Sarah McMullin announced updates enhancing data openness, AI insights, and sustainability. A highlight was the introduction of **Gemini Cloud Assist**, an AI-driven tool designed to provide immediate insights into cloud costs and optimization opportunities.

### Microsoft Azure

Jimin Li detailed enhancements to cost estimation tools that enable engineers to access detailed cost estimates before deploying resources. Microsoft Fabric was presented as a unified platform for cost data analytics, with AI-powered Co-Pilot advancing decision-making capabilities.

### AWS

James Greenfield announced the general availability of cost optimization recommendations for RDS instances and introduced AWS FOCUS 1.0 data export for seamless integration across cloud providers.

## The Future of FinOps

Day 2 showcased meaningful advancements in FinOps practices. The overarching themes pointed toward deeper integration between finance and technology teams, AI-driven cost management capabilities, and an increasing emphasis on sustainability as a core FinOps objective.
