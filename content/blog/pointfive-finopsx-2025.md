## WasteBusters: Airborne!

PointFive hosted an evening event at the San Diego Air and Space Museum during FinOps X 2025. The gathering brought together FinOps practitioners, cloud engineers, and industry professionals for an evening of networking and discussion.

## An Energizing Evening

Attendees had the opportunity to exchange industry experiences, discuss FinOps best practices, and collaborate on solutions addressing cloud spending inefficiencies. The unique venue setting provided an energizing backdrop for conversations about the future of cloud cost management.

## Thank You

Thank you to everyone who joined for the event. The energy and insights shared throughout the evening were invaluable, and PointFive looks forward to continuing these conversations at future gatherings.
