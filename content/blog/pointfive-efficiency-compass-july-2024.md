Welcome to the July 2024 edition of the PointFive Efficiency Compass — our monthly roundup of cloud optimization insights, product updates, and industry trends.

## What's New This Month

### Platform Updates

July brought continued improvements to PointFive's platform, including enhanced detection capabilities across AWS services and improvements to our remediation workflows. Engineering teams can now take action on more optimization opportunities directly from their existing tools.

### Industry Highlights

The cloud optimization landscape continued to evolve in July 2024, with increasing focus on AI workload costs and the growing importance of engineering-driven efficiency practices. FinOps teams are increasingly partnering with engineering to move beyond visibility toward continuous optimization.

### Looking Ahead

We're preparing exciting product updates for the fall season. Stay tuned for announcements around multi-cloud analytics, expanded Kubernetes support, and new automation capabilities.
