Welcome to the June 2024 edition of the PointFive Efficiency Compass — our monthly roundup of cloud optimization insights, product updates, and industry trends.

## What's New This Month

### Platform Updates

This month we continued to expand our detection coverage and analytics capabilities. Key updates include improvements to our cost anomaly detection, enhanced multi-cloud support, and new dashboard features for tracking optimization progress across teams.

### Industry Trends

June 2024 brought significant developments in cloud cost optimization and FinOps practices. Organizations continued to deepen their commitment to cloud efficiency, with growing recognition that cost optimization requires engineering-driven approaches beyond simple visibility.

### Community

We're grateful for the growing community of FinOps practitioners and cloud engineers who contribute to making cloud efficiency a shared priority. Stay tuned for more insights in next month's edition.
