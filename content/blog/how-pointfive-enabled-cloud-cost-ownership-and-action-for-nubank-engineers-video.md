## Empowering Engineers at Nubank

PointFive CEO Alon Arvatz presented alongside Mike Rosenberg, Senior Director of Engineering at Nubank, to discuss how PointFive helped Nubank maximize cloud investments by inspiring accountability among engineers.

## The Partnership

PointFive partnered with Nubank to enhance cloud cost efficiency across their engineering organization. The collaboration focused on enabling engineers to take better control over their cloud cost efficiency -- moving beyond centralized FinOps oversight toward distributed engineering ownership.

## Key Outcomes

Through the partnership, Nubank's engineering teams gained improved ability to:

- **Understand cloud cost inefficiencies** at the level where they could take direct action
- **Solve problems faster and more confidently** with the context needed to prioritize optimizations
- **Take ownership** of their cloud spending as part of their engineering responsibilities
