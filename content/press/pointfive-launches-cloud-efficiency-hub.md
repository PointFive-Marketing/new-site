**New York, September 15, 2025** — PointFive, a cloud cost optimization platform, today announced the launch of the Cloud Efficiency Hub, a centralized dashboard that gives engineering teams real-time visibility into optimization progress, savings achieved, and waste prevention across their entire cloud environment.

## Introducing the Cloud Efficiency Hub

The Cloud Efficiency Hub provides a single pane of glass for tracking cloud optimization health. Engineering and FinOps teams can monitor savings as they accumulate, view waste detected and prevented over time, and track optimization health scores across services, accounts, and teams.

CEO and co-founder Yoav Toussia-Cohen noted that customers have been asking for a way to measure and communicate the impact of their optimization efforts, and the Cloud Efficiency Hub gives every stakeholder a clear, real-time view of progress.

## Key Capabilities

The Cloud Efficiency Hub delivers several core capabilities designed to keep engineering teams aligned on optimization goals. Real-time savings tracking shows cumulative and per-service savings as optimizations take effect. Waste detection dashboards surface newly identified inefficiencies alongside waste that has been prevented through previously implemented recommendations.

Optimization health scores provide a single metric per service, account, or team, making it easy to identify areas that need attention. Historical trend views allow teams to track improvement over time and demonstrate ROI to leadership.

## Workflow Integrations

The Cloud Efficiency Hub integrates directly with existing engineering workflows. Teams can push optimization alerts and progress updates to Slack channels, create and track Jira tickets for recommended actions, and configure custom notifications based on savings thresholds or new waste detection events. These integrations ensure that optimization insights reach engineers where they already work, reducing friction and accelerating time to action.

## Availability

The Cloud Efficiency Hub is available immediately to all existing PointFive customers at no additional cost. New customers gain access to the Hub as part of the standard PointFive platform onboarding.

## About PointFive

PointFive specializes in cloud cost optimization and waste identification using its proprietary DeepWaste Detection Engine, built on cyber-inspired methodologies and advanced AI. The platform provides actionable insights and continuous management solutions that foster collaboration between FinOps and engineering teams.
