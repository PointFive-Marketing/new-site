**New York, NY** — PointFive, a cloud efficiency solutions provider, has been selected by Redpoint Ventures for the 2025 InfraRed 100 List. The recognition highlights 100 emerging private companies in cloud infrastructure and validates PointFive's work in pioneering Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM).

## A New Standard for Cloud Efficiency

Founder and CPO Ben David expressed pride in the recognition, describing it as an honor and noting that PointFive was founded on the conviction that cloud efficiency had to evolve into a continuous practice.

## The Problem with Traditional Tools

Traditional Cloud Cost Management (CCM) tools typically focus on financial reporting and surface-level optimizations while overlooking deeper technical inefficiencies. These tools rely on generic cost alerts rather than contextual, remediation-focused signals, leaving teams without clear action steps and reducing engineering team accountability.

## CEPM: A Category-Defining Approach

PointFive developed CEPM as a new category of cloud optimization technology, inspired by Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM). CEPM represents a fundamental shift from periodic cost reviews to a continuous, strategic discipline for managing cloud efficiency across an organization.

## Cloud Efficiency Hub

PointFive has also created the Cloud Efficiency Hub, described as the industry's most comprehensive library of cloud cost inefficiencies. The Hub features contributions from 42 contributors and documents 132 inefficiencies across IaaS, PaaS, and related environments.

## About PointFive

PointFive delivers cloud efficiency solutions for enterprise engineering and FinOps teams, providing deep insights, continuous optimization, and measurable outcomes.
