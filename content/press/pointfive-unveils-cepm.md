PointFive has announced the launch of Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM), a new cloud optimization technology category designed for enterprise engineering and FinOps teams. The approach represents a shift from periodic cost reviews to continuous efficiency management, drawing inspiration from the Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM) model.

## Addressing a Gap in Cloud Cost Management

Traditional cloud cost management tools focus primarily on financial reporting and surface-level optimizations, often neglecting deeper technical inefficiencies embedded in cloud infrastructure. CEPM addresses this gap by proactively detecting hidden cloud waste and delivering actionable recommendations that engineering teams can act on immediately.

## Key Platform Capabilities

- **Deep Optimization Insights** — Identifies subtle inefficiencies that traditional tools miss
- **Seamless Workflow Integration** — Connects with Jira, ServiceNow, Slack, and Teams
- **Rapid ROI** — Demonstrates significant cost savings within weeks of deployment
- **Frictionless Deployment** — Agentless setup through a single integration
- **Multi-Cloud Coverage** — Expanded optimization across AWS, GCP, and Microsoft Azure, with unique Azure-specific insights

## Leadership Perspective

CEO Alon Arvatz emphasized that cloud efficiency should not be a periodic task or reactive exercise, and that CEPM empowers engineering teams to continuously embed efficiency into their daily operations.

VP of Research Dor Azouri noted that traditional tools overlook platform-specific metrics because their focus remains on surface-level visibility, which is the gap CEPM is designed to close.

## Enterprise Adoption

Customers including Blackhawk Network, E.ON, Elastic, and Fanatics have reported increased efficiency and significant cost savings within weeks of deploying the CEPM platform.
