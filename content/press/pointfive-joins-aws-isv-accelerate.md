**Tel Aviv, July 31, 2024** — PointFive, a cloud cost optimization platform for enterprise companies specializing in uncovering deeper, typically overlooked cloud waste, announced that the company has officially joined the AWS ISV Accelerate Program.

## About the AWS ISV Accelerate Program

The AWS ISV Accelerate Program is a co-sell program for organizations that provide software solutions running on or integrating with AWS. The program facilitates better customer outcomes through mutual commitment between AWS and its partners.

## Strategic Significance

CEO and co-founder Alon Arvatz described the inclusion as a significant milestone in PointFive's journey to provide deep, actionable analysis and recommendations for continuous cloud management and optimization.

## Partnership Benefits

Through the program, PointFive gains co-sell support and benefits to meet customer needs in collaboration with AWS field sellers globally. The partnership also enables PointFive to expand its reach, helping more businesses uncover hidden cost savings, increase efficiency, and access more robust tools for managing cloud investments.

## Technology Differentiators

PointFive is the only solution to apply an agentless approach to both AWS and Kubernetes integrations, providing advanced observability and optimization opportunities for services such as DynamoDB, EKS, S3, and more. The proprietary DeepWaste Technology uncovers significant savings potential hidden within cloud environments.

## About PointFive

PointFive redefines cloud cost management with an end-to-end platform that uncovers deep, infrastructure-level savings opportunities. The platform enables enterprise organizations to promote continuous cloud cost efficiency through actionable and collaborative remediation workflows. PointFive is backed by investors including Index Ventures, Entree Capital, Sheva Capital, and Vesey Ventures.
