PointFive, the pioneer of Cloud Efficiency Posture Management (CEPM), has announced a strategic partnership with XIBIX Solutions GmbH, a German technology company specializing in cloud-native development, IT consulting, and managed services. The collaboration aims to help companies optimize their digital transformation by improving the efficiency of their cloud systems.

## Expanding European Reach

Akiva Kates, VP of Partnerships at PointFive, described the partnership as a significant step in expanding PointFive's presence within the European market, noting that XIBIX's commitment to innovation aligns with PointFive's mission to optimize cloud expenditures.

Michael Solbach, Managing Director at XIBIX, said that in PointFive they found a partner that helps customers rapidly identify optimization potential, delivering results at the highest level.

## Partnership Value

The collaboration brings PointFive's CEPM platform to the European market with localized expertise and direct support for German and broader European clients, building on the company's existing enterprise customer success in the region.

## Recent Industry Recognition

PointFive has earned notable recognition alongside the partnership:

- Named to the **2025 InfraRed 100** by Redpoint Ventures, a list of the 100 most promising cloud infrastructure companies
- Debuted in the **2025 G2 Summer Reports** with a #6 Relationship Index ranking
