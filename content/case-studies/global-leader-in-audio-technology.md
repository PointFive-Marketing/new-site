## Overview

**Client:** Global Leader in Audio Technology
**Industry:** Audio-Visual Technology
**Cloud Provider:** AWS
**Challenge:** Basic cost reporting that couldn't identify architectural waste or facilitate cross-team collaboration

## The Challenge

The organization, a 50-year leader in audio technology supporting both Hollywood production and consumer platforms, developed extensive AWS infrastructure serving multiple engineering teams across regions. Growth in their cloud footprint created pressure to move beyond simple budget maintenance toward systematic waste reduction.

Their initial standalone reporting application monitored spending but couldn't detect architectural waste. The company launched a dedicated FinOps program emphasizing continuous efficiency and infrastructure optimization, requiring deeper visibility than their existing solution provided.

Key challenges included:

- **Limited detection depth** — Basic cost reporting missed complex waste patterns embedded in infrastructure
- **Fragmented tooling** — Teams relied on multiple disconnected tools for different aspects of cost management
- **Cross-team coordination** — No unified platform to facilitate collaboration across engineering teams and regions
- **Lack of actionable context** — Recommendations lacked the technical detail needed for confident implementation

## The Solution

PointFive delivered a unified platform that consolidated reporting and optimization capabilities, eliminating tool fragmentation:

- **DeepWaste Detection Engine** — Surfaced hidden inefficiencies that previous tools completely overlooked
- **Analytics Module** — Delivered flexible cost tracking with custom visualizations and trend analysis
- **Opportunities Module** — Categorized savings by type and account for scalable remediation
- **Remediation Guidance** — Provided actionable recommendations with complexity assessments and integration into engineering workflows via Jira and ServiceNow
- **1-Click Remediation** — Enabled rapid infrastructure adjustments with ready-to-use scripts

Teams gained visibility into system-wide inefficiencies, enabling coordinated action across regions. The platform's consolidated recommendations simplified stakeholder decision-making globally.
