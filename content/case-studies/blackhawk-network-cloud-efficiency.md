## Overview

**Client:** Blackhawk Network (BHN)
**Industry:** Financial Services / Payments
**Cloud Provider:** AWS
**Challenge:** Growing cloud costs across a complex payments infrastructure

Blackhawk Network, a global leader in branded payments and incentive solutions, operates a cloud infrastructure that supports billions of dollars in transactions annually. As cloud spend grew alongside business expansion, the team needed a more effective approach to identifying and eliminating waste without disrupting critical payment processing systems.

## The Challenge

BHN's cloud environment supports high-availability payment processing with strict performance and compliance requirements. Traditional cost optimization approaches were too blunt for this context -- generic recommendations to downsize instances or eliminate resources required extensive investigation before anyone could act on them with confidence.

The team faced several specific challenges:

- **High-stakes remediation** -- Any changes to production infrastructure needed validated safety guarantees
- **Cross-team coordination** -- Optimization opportunities spanned multiple engineering teams with different priorities
- **Limited engineering bandwidth** -- Engineers needed clear, actionable recommendations rather than raw data
- **Complex service dependencies** -- Interconnected services made it difficult to assess the impact of changes

## The Solution

BHN deployed PointFive to provide continuous efficiency monitoring with the engineering context needed to act safely and quickly. The platform's approach addressed their core concerns:

**Contextual recommendations** -- Each finding included ownership attribution, workload context, and validated remediation steps, enabling engineers to assess and implement changes with confidence.

**Workflow integration** -- Recommendations were routed directly to responsible teams through existing ticketing and communication tools, reducing the coordination overhead that had previously slowed optimization efforts.

**Safe remediation** -- PointFive's recommendations included risk assessment and rollback procedures, critical for an environment where availability directly impacts revenue.

## Results

Within weeks of deployment, PointFive identified meaningful savings opportunities that previous tools had not surfaced, while providing the safety guarantees BHN required for their critical payments infrastructure.

- **Rapid time-to-value** with significant savings identified in the first weeks
- **Engineering adoption** driven by trusted, context-rich recommendations
- **Continuous discovery** of new optimization opportunities as infrastructure evolves
- **Compliance-friendly** approach that respects the constraints of regulated environments

## Conclusion

For organizations operating critical infrastructure, cloud cost optimization must balance efficiency with safety. PointFive's contextual, engineering-focused approach enabled BHN to achieve meaningful savings without compromising the reliability their business demands.
